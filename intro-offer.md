# New-Client 60-Minute RMT Visit

The first 60-minute RMT appointment at Kimura Massage Therapy & Rehab is $109 + HST for eligible new clients. The regular 60-minute price is $120 + HST.

The visit includes health intake, consent, goal-setting, assessment as appropriate, hands-on massage treatment, and aftercare discussion. Cupping and hot towels may be included when clinically appropriate, wanted by the client, and time permits. No package is required.

The appointment is performed by Ricky Arora, Registered Massage Therapist. An RMT receipt is provided. Direct billing may be available when the client's plan and insurer approve the claim.
