## Editorial standard

Articles are educational, avoid guaranteed outcomes, and distinguish massage from diagnosis or medical treatment. Safety advice prioritizes appropriate assessment for severe, unexplained, traumatic, neurological, vascular, or systemic symptoms. Pressure and treatment choices require ongoing consent.
