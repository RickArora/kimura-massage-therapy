Why people book

You do not need a dramatic injury to need care.

Most people book because a small issue keeps showing up: a stiff neck after work, a low back that feels guarded, shoulder tension from training, or hips that feel locked after sitting.

Desk and phone tension

Long sitting, screen work, and forward head posture can make the neck, upper traps, chest, and mid back feel overloaded.

Gym and sport tightness

Training can leave calves, quads, hips, shoulders, and low back feeling stiff when recovery does not keep up.

Stress holding patterns

Stress can show up as jaw clenching, shallow breathing, raised shoulders, and muscles that never fully relax.
