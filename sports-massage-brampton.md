# Sports Massage in Brampton

Sports massage is massage therapy planned around a person's training, activity, recovery needs, and schedule. A session may focus on areas that feel tired or restricted, but it does not guarantee faster healing or improved performance.

Ricky Arora, RMT, asks about the activity, current symptoms, recent training load, relevant health history, and the timing of the next event or workout. Pressure and techniques are adapted to the goal. Treatment close to competition is usually approached conservatively.
