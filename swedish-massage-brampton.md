# Swedish Massage in Brampton

Swedish massage generally uses flowing strokes, kneading, and comfortable pressure. Clients often choose it for relaxation, general muscle tension, or a first massage appointment. Pressure can remain light or be adjusted within the client's comfort; the label does not determine one fixed treatment.

Treatment is provided by Ricky Arora, Registered Massage Therapist, after an intake and consent discussion. The client controls treatment areas, draping, positioning, and pressure and may change or withdraw consent at any time.
